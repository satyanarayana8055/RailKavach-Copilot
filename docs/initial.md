4. What we submitted (our idea)

Idea: RailKavach Copilot, a Gemini-powered safety assistant. Indian Railways officers ask, in Hindi or English, what went wrong on any train run, and get answers with evidence from the cab radio, cab video and wagon camera data RailKavach already processes.
Problem: safety review is still manual. At NWR so far: 11,354 audio files, 3.68 million transcript lines, 1,583 loco pilots. Officers still read dashboards and transcripts line by line, so investigations are slow and patterns are missed.
Solution: a conversational layer on top of RailKavach. It searches radio transcripts, cab-video alerts (phone use, smoking, drowsiness) and wagon-damage logs. It summarises each incident with timestamps and evidence clips, drafts the incident report and crew briefing, and flags repeat patterns by crew, section or time of day. No new hardware.
Users: safety officers, crew controllers and operations teams (Sr DEE/TRO) in each division, and zonal safety cells; later metro, freight terminals, ports and mining sidings.
Google stack: Gemini API (long-context, multimodal Q&A with timestamp citations; Hindi/English summaries and reports) and Gemma (on-premise option). Prototype in Google AI Studio, then Vertex AI behind Cloud Run, BigQuery with vector search, and Cloud Storage for audio and video evidence.
Business model: per-division add-on to the RailKavach contract, zone-wide licences, and partners for metro, ports and overseas railways.
Stage: MVP (the RailKavach base is live; the copilot layer is new).
